WALTON COLLEGE PROFILE | CV

I am an economist and the ConocoPhillips Chair in International Economics and Business at the Sam M. Walton College of Business, University of Arkansas, Fayetteville. I am an NBER research associate, a J-PAL affiliate and an AALIMS faculty fellow, as well as an Indonesia Project Associate at the Australian National University (ANU). My research focuses on empirical microeconomic topics related to human/social capital and urban economics in low-middle-income countries. I have papers published or forthcoming in, among others, The American Economic Review, AER: Insights, AEJ: Applied Economics, and The Review of Economics and Statistics.
