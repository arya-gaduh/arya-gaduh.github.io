Arya Gaduh is an applied economist interested in how policy shapes economic outcomes. His current research focuses on human/social capital, and urban economics.

His research has been published in leading journals in economics, including American Economic Review, AER: Insights, AEJ: Applied Economics, Review of Economics and Statistics, and The Economic Journal. Most of his research has focused on Indonesia, where he is from. Thankfully, his wonderful co-authors have lured him out of his comfort zone into new research settings.
