econ 5783 - applied microeconometrics

Fall Semester 2021 -

This course covers the principles of causal inference. Methods include panel data models, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity designs, difference-in-differences, and matching. Emphasis on developing a solid understanding of the underlying econometric principles of the methods taught as well as on their empirical application.

Textbook

Angrist and Pischke (2015). Mastering Metrics

econ 6843 - international development economics II

Spring Semester

This is a graduate-level course on the microeconomics of development. Students are presumed to have general knowledge about development and the major policy issues. Its related objective is to help student develop their empirical skills for their research. Good working knowledge of microeconomic theory and econometrics is necessary.

econ 6613 - econometrics I

Fall Semester 2013-2019

This course offers a rigorous, but intuitive, graduate-level introduction to econometrics that provides a foundation for users of econometrics to begin engaging in applied research and to take more advanced coursework.

Textbooks

Greene, W. (2012). Econometric Analysis, 7E (main)

Wooldridge, J. (2010). Econometric Analysis of Cross Section and Panel Data, 2E (supp.)
