Honors thesis

Interested in working with me?

My research focuses on evaluating policies and addressing policy-relevant questions using modern econometrics. If you are considering an empirical thesis in a related area, email me to discuss your idea and whether it would be a good fit.

In your first email, include

  • A short description of your question and why it interests you.
  • Relevant economics, statistics, or econometrics coursework, and any experience working with data.
  • Your anticipated graduation date and thesis timeline. In general, I only supervise students who approach me before their graduation year.

You do not need a fully developed proposal to start a conversation.

Recommendation
letters

Ask before listing me as a recommender

When you get in touch, please remind me how we have worked together, such as a course you took with me, an honors thesis I advised, or a research project we worked on.

As a general rule, recommendation letters are strongest when they come from professors who know your work well, often through courses in which you performed strongly.

Please contact me well before your first deadline so I can confirm whether I can write a letter.

Include with your request

  • Your CV and a brief description of what you are applying for.
  • The courses or projects you completed with me, including the semester.
  • A list of programs or opportunities, deadlines, and submission instructions.
  • A draft statement or relevant writing sample, if available.
