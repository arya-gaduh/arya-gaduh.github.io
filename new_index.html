I study how social context, such as religion, culture and social networks, interacts with economic incentives to affect behavior in developing countries. To this end, I employ empirical and experimental methods to examine how economic incentives mold the social context and, vice versa, how social context influences individual incentives and behavior.

I am also interested in general microeconomic development questions, in particular, related to health and education.
