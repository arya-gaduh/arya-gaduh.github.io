development resources

data

The World Bank Microdata Library
Data collected by the World Bank through sample surveys of households, business establishments or other facilities.

International Household Survey Network
An informal network of international agencies that aims to improve the availability, accessibility, and quality of survey data within developing countries.

Association of Religion Data Archives
Free access on quality data on religion.

IPUMS International
Integrated international microdata based on national censuses.

Indonesia Family Life Survey
Longitudinal dataset of Indonesian households.

INDO-DAPOER
Province- and district-level economic data for Indonesia. Part of the World Bank open database.

BPS-Statistics
Indonesia's statistical agency. This link details datasets available to purchase from the agency.
